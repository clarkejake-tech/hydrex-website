Surfaces We Clean

Deep Cleaning for Exterior Hard Surfaces.

Over autumn and winter, British garden paving and paths become coated in slippery biofilms. Our surface cleaning lifts surface soil and restores traction.

  • Natural Stone Patios: Indian sandstone, limestone, porcelain, and slate terraces
  • Block Paving: Driveways, paths, and borders
  • Pathways & Steps: Remove dangerous slippery algae on walkways
  • Surface Rotary Cleaning: Reduces stripes and overspray on surrounding borders
Realistic Expectation:

We thoroughly clean accumulated surface grime, algae, and organic staining. We do not promise magical restoration to "brand new condition" where original stone has naturally weathered, aged, or cracked over decades.
